# Make the Judge Fall in Love in One Line

An entry to the make.fun bounty
[Make the Judge Fall in Love in One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-deranalabs-make-the-judge-fall-in-love-in-one-line-8e712c13).

`main.py` writes one line, at most 240 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`.
Python 3.13, standard library only, no network.

## The line

> The voicemail is eleven seconds: her breathing, a dog barking, her saying nothing. Four years. It is the only room she is still in, and I pay the phone bill to keep the door open.

## How it was chosen

Fourteen candidate lines were written, then sixty more, and each was read
against the bounty's published judge brief in a full round robin: every pair
judged twice, once in each order, with a margin from 1 to 5, so a decisive win
counts more than a narrow one. The surviving lines were carried into two more
rounds and the finalists were re-run head to head. This line beat the night-bus
line 1.40 to 0.60 and the earlier voicemail line 1.50 to 0.50.

## Run

```
python3 main.py
```
